# Model Selection (Phase 4)

**Chosen:** `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` embeddings + `jinaai/jina-reranker-v1-turbo-en`
reranker, reranking the top 20 fused passages at 1,200 characters each. These are the
defaults in `models.py` and `search.py`.

## Setup

- **Wiki:** Politics, 414 pages plus 101 raw text files, 4,673 chunks.
- **Questions:** 50, generated by Claude from pages sampled with
  `wiki eval sample --seed 7` plus 12 random entity and document pages. 35 single-page,
  10 two-page (along a derived relation), 5 unanswerable. 13 tune, 37 test. Stored with the
  wiki as `eval/questions.yaml`.
- **Machine:** Ryzen 7 9800X3D, CPU only, fastembed 0.8.1 (ONNX Runtime 1.30).
- **Metrics:** hit@1 and hit@3 (any listed answer page at that rank), MRR, two-page
  questions with both pages in the top 5, query latency excluding model load.

## Findings

- **The reranker matters, the embedding model doesn't.** Once a reranker runs, all three
  embedding models land within one question of each other. The two larger models cost 4×
  and 17× the embedding time for no gain.
- **jina-turbo over MiniLM-L12:** the same test hit@3 (0.82), better hit@1, MRR and
  two-page coverage, and about 30% faster. On the 13 tune questions MiniLM scored higher
  hit@3 (0.67 against 0.50), a difference of two questions; the larger test split did not
  confirm it.
- **Reranker scores separate answerable from unanswerable questions.** For jina-turbo,
  the median top score is +0.70 for answerable questions against −1.21 for unanswerable
  ones (MiniLM: +5.3 against −5.0). A "nothing relevant" threshold could use this later.
- **Excluding the dossier `claims.md` ledgers from the index** made no measurable
  difference (MRR 0.655 against 0.652) and would break the claim pages' links back to
  their ledger, so the ledgers stay indexed.

## Rerank depth

bge-small + jina-turbo, all 50 questions:

| Passages reranked | Characters each | Hit@1 | Hit@3 | MRR | ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 2,000 | 0.53 | 0.73 | 0.65 | 779 |
| 30 | 1,200 | 0.53 | 0.76 | 0.66 | 546 |
| **20** | **1,200** | 0.51 | **0.80** | 0.64 | **330** |
| 15 | 1,200 | 0.51 | 0.73 | 0.62 | 275 |
| 40 | 1,200 | 0.53 | 0.71 | 0.65 | 693 |

20 × 1,200 is 2.4× faster than the original setting at equal quality within noise (the
differences are one or two questions).

## Caveats

- 50 questions is small: one question moves hit@1 by 0.02. Treat differences under
  about 0.05 as noise.
- Questions were written from the pages they target, even though paraphrased. Real
  questions may be harder.
- Rerun with `wiki eval run --split test` after large wiki changes or before switching
  models.
